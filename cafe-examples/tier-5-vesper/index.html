No. 01 — The room

Fourteen seats.
One menu.
All night.

We took on a seventeenth-century vaulted room on Victoria Street and stripped it back to stone. No piped music. No theatre. Just the work — two tasting menus each evening, built from what arrived that morning.

The kitchen changes the menu every six weeks. The wine list changes more often. The candles have been the same since 2014.

"The city goes to sleep and Vesper opens. There's something right about that." — The Observer Food Monthly

No. 04 — The cellar

700 bottles,
one philosophy.

Our cellar is small by restaurant standards. Every bottle is here because someone on the team has tasted it, liked it, and can talk about it. No catalogue wine, no inflated margins.

Wine pairing is a collaboration between you and the sommelier, built around what you're eating and what you enjoy.
