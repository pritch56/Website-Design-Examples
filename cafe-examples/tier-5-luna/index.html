Our Story

A little corner of calm in the heart of Exeter

Luna Cafe was born from a simple belief: that nourishing yourself should feel joyful, not complicated. Katie and Phil opened Luna on Queen Street to bring Exeter a truly health-forward space — somewhere you can slow down, sip something divine, and feel genuinely well.

Everything on our menu is gluten-free, vegetarian, and made with ingredients that love you back.
