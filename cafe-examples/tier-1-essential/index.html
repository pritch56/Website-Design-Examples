03 — Hello there

We're Ros & Tom,
and this is our café.

We met at a bakery in Lisbon in 2014, came home and opened Daisy & Bean five years later in a shop that used to be a florist. The name comes from the flowers we kept finding in the floorboards.

Everything we serve is something we'd want to eat ourselves on a slow morning. We change the menu when the weather changes. We close on Mondays so we can rest. We hope you'll come in.

— Ros & Tom
